# פריטים שתוקנו, למדידה חוזרת. בחר את האפשרות המשלימה נכון.
# "___ … ___" פירושו שני חסרים; האפשרות מספקת את שתי המילים בסדרן.

1. The clerk had already added up the totals; yet an outside panel was told to ___ every contract for hidden fees.
   A) scrutinize
   B) interpret
   C) compile
   D) survey

2. The regions sent their reports on time; therefore, the ministry has no right to ___ the promised funds.
   A) refuse
   B) withhold
   C) deny
   D) retain

3. Although the office may ___ the fund day to day, paying each bill itself, only the council can ___ how much goes to housing.
   A) administer + determine
   B) determine + administer
   C) oversee + determine
   D) administer + assess

4. Because the fund must ___ reserves while trade is strong, it can ___ them without new taxes once demand falls.
   A) accumulate + exhaust
   B) accumulate + spend
   C) spend + accumulate
   D) deplete + spend

5. Once a single tube is left open, dust can ___ the culture, and the analyst must then ___ the whole batch from the analysis.
   A) contaminate + spoil
   B) contaminate + exclude
   C) exclude + contaminate
   D) taint + exclude

6. Certain quantities, such as the boiling point of a new liquid, are impossible to ___ from theory alone, and each must be measured.
   A) obtain
   B) extract
   C) derive
   D) isolate

7. The mason will ___ the name into the lintel; furthermore, the gilder will ___ each letter so that it shines from the courtyard.
   A) engrave + adorn
   B) adorn + engrave
   C) engrave + conceal
   D) portray + adorn

8. Judges who ___ with the majority sign the main opinion; those who ___ from it, however, must file a separate note.
   A) concur + dissent
   B) dissent + concur
   C) comply + dissent
   D) concur + diverge

9. Although one of the two authors had once been her student, the reviewer stayed ___ and weighed every claim in both papers by the same standard.
   A) courteous
   B) cautious
   C) candid
   D) impartial

10. His argument had already convinced the committee, and moreover his speech was so ___ that listeners repeated its phrases word for word for days.
   A) eloquent
   B) persuasive
   C) vivid
   D) fluent

11. The beans need salt and a little pepper; in addition, a spoon of vinegar will ___ their flavor.
   A) enhance
   B) exaggerate
   C) enlarge
   D) inflate

12. Because the two maps came from one survey, their coastlines should ___ when one sheet is laid over the other; where they ___, a clerk has made a copying error.
   A) align + combine
   B) align + differ
   C) differ + align
   D) combine + differ

13. He sold the whole farm to the new owners; even so, the contract let him ___ the narrow path to the well.
   A) retain
   B) occupy
   C) maintain
   D) borrow

14. Some building materials, such as sun-dried brick and cheap roof tile, are so ___ that one light knock breaks them.
   A) delicate
   B) stiff
   C) hollow
   D) fragile

15. Because the ice on the high ridge begins to ___ each spring, the streams below ___ above their banks and flood the low fields.
   A) rise + melt
   B) freeze + rise
   C) melt + rise
   D) melt + drain

16. Because one crate carries the whole order, the packers ___ each plate in straw and only then ___ the heavy lid with eight long screws.
   A) bend + fasten
   B) fasten + wrap
   C) wrap + fasten
   D) wrap + bend

17. Although the valve and the pipe are cut from the same steel, the valve must ___ the flow at once and the pipe must ___ pressure for years.
   A) absorb + withstand
   B) halt + withstand
   C) withstand + halt
   D) halt + absorb

18. The choir sang the first verse alone; yet a quiet harp then began to ___ the voices without repeating their notes.
   A) echo
   B) imitate
   C) accompany
   D) replace

19. Because the room had no door at all, every step in the corridor was enough to ___ the readers, who kept losing their place on the page.
   A) disturb
   B) confuse
   C) interrupt
   D) distract

20. Every line of the tune comes back unchanged; therefore, the ___ makes the words stick after a single hearing.
   A) tone
   B) rhythm
   C) rhyme
   D) repetition

21. The job needs a strong body, and it also needs a ___ that takes years to learn.
   A) talent
   B) quality
   C) skill
   D) experience

22. Each line of the test was a short ___, such as who wrote the book or where the river begins.
   A) example
   B) detail
   C) answer
   D) question

23. Set a pot of water on the fire, and once it is hot, ___ the wild leaves for ten minutes before you add any salt or oil.
   A) boil
   B) burn
   C) melt
   D) roast

24. The wedding hall was small; yet my parents invited every ___ who lived within a day's drive.
   A) neighbor
   B) relative
   C) colleague
   D) visitor

25. Speed alone does not win a long race, because running hour after hour demands ___ that no short sprint ever tests.
   A) muscle
   B) strength
   C) stamina
   D) talent

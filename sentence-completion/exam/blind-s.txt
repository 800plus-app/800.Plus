# 59 sentence-completion items. Choose the ONE option that best completes each sentence.
# "___ ... ___" means two blanks: the option supplies both words, in order.

1. Everyone understood the rules and nobody found a mistake in them, but no child could ___ the puzzle.
   A) explain
   B) solve
   C) correct
   D) answer

2. He did not give up the theory; he chose only to ___ it from the journal until the new data arrived.
   A) abandon
   B) withdraw
   C) dismiss
   D) reject

3. Although both come from one workshop, the valve must ___ the flow at once and the pipe must ___ pressure for years.
   A) halt … withstand
   B) withstand … halt
   C) absorb … withstand
   D) halt … absorb

4. Until the office finds a new sponsor, it cannot ___ the water bill, and the service will stop.
   A) sell
   B) pay
   C) spend
   D) save

5. If someone leaves the gate of the zoo open, a monkey can ___ and never come back.
   A) escape
   B) run
   C) fall
   D) jump

6. Because the trader kept selling goods barred by the new rule, the office moved to ___ his permit for good.
   A) suspend
   B) revoke
   C) cancel
   D) restrict

7. Because the handle must stay cool in the hand, the maker chose plastic: bare metal would ___ the heat straight to the fingers.
   A) conduct
   B) reflect
   C) transmit
   D) absorb

8. Because the storm ruined a whole year of fruit, the growers asked the council to ___ them for the loss.
   A) compensate
   B) reward
   C) insure
   D) refund

9. The old recipe uses only local ___, for instance wheat from the valley and olives from the hill behind the house.
   A) material
   B) ingredients
   C) product
   D) substance

10. Since the wording is not wrong, only out of date, the council agrees to ___ the clause rather than replace it entirely.
   A) correct
   B) amend
   C) repair
   D) restore

11. Although the two boards use one rulebook, the stricter of them may ___ a request that the other would ___ without debate.
   A) grant … refuse

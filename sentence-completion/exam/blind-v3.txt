# 34 sentence-completion items. Choose the ONE option that best completes each sentence.
# Two blanks (___ ... ___) means the option supplies both words, in order.

1. Climbers carry heavy ___, such as ropes, metal hooks and a lamp for the last hours of the night.
   A) device
   B) equipment
   C) machine
   D) material

2. Although the teacher said the word aloud twice, the small child still could not ___ it without help.
   A) answer
   B) copy
   C) continue
   D) repeat

3. Until the office finds a new sponsor, it cannot ___ the water bill, and the service will stop.
   A) sell
   B) pay
   C) save
   D) spend

4. Since the wording is not wrong, only out of date, the council agrees to ___ the clause rather than replace it entirely.
   A) correct
   B) repair
   C) restore
   D) amend

5. Once the last form is stamped, the court can ___ the contract that both sides have already agreed to in full.
   A) obey
   B) impose
   C) dictate
   D) enforce

6. Until the missing log is found, the office cannot ___ the exact hour at which the alarm was first raised.
   A) estimate
   B) ascertain
   C) recall
   D) confirm

7. Although the vase had been glued twice already, one light knock was enough to ___ it into a dozen pieces.
   A) crack
   B) fracture
   C) shatter
   D) bend

8. The hall was too small; furthermore, the ___ the club set for the season did not allow renting a larger one.
   A) price
   B) cost
   C) budget
   D) expense

9. Unless the roof is fixed before the rain, the leak will cause serious ___ to the wooden floor.
   A) harm
   B) damage
   C) ruin
   D) injury

10. Because the storm ruined a whole year of fruit, the growers asked the council to ___ them for the loss.
   A) refund
   B) insure
   C) reward
   D) compensate

11. The soup needs salt and a little pepper; likewise, a spoon of vinegar will ___ the flavor of the beans.
   A) inflate
   B) enhance
   C) enlarge
   D) exaggerate

12. Because only three people signed up, the club decided to ___ the meeting and give back the money.
   A) close
   B) cancel
   C) delete
   D) remove

13. The fog did not stop the search altogether; even so, it was enough to ___ the volunteers, who reached the ridge two hours late.
   A) prevent
   B) halt
   C) block
   D) hinder

14. A skilled teacher can ___ the whole idea in a single hour; yet a pupil may need a year to ___ it fully.
   A) instill … absorb
   B) impart … absorb
   C) impart … obtain
   D) absorb … impart

15. Everyone understood the rules and nobody found a mistake in them, but no child could ___ the puzzle.
   A) solve
   B) explain
   C) correct
   D) answer

16. The big room was empty and every step was heard twice; each word also came back as a clear ___.
   A) echo
   B) noise
   C) voice
   D) tone

17. Each clerk counted the crates twice and made no error; furthermore, neither of them could explain the ___ between the two totals.
   A) mistake
   B) conflict
   C) contradiction
   D) discrepancy

18. He had defended the figure for a whole hour; even so, once the audit proved him wrong, he agreed to ___ the argument.
   A) concede
   B) surrender
   C) forfeit
   D) abandon

19. Some hardy shrubs, such as the thorn and the wild olive, ___ on bare slopes and still bear fruit every single year.
   A) survive
   B) persist
   C) linger
   D) thrive

20. Although the plan was reasonable, the council decided to ___ it after a single short debate.
   A) forbid
   B) resist
   C) reject
   D) deny

21. The tribe kept no written history; however, its elders could ___ every name in the family line without a single mistake.
   A) retrieve
   B) recall
   C) recognize
   D) memorize

22. Because the river rose for days, nothing of the old bridge was left; the water managed to ___ it completely.
   A) break
   B) destroy
   C) burn
   D) damage

23. She checks the lock twice every night, and she also bites her nails; each is a small ___ she cannot explain.
   A) reflex
   B) tradition
   C) obsession
   D) habit

24. Because the team trained through the winter, it was able to ___ every goal it had set in autumn.
   A) achieve
   B) earn
   C) gain
   D) win

25. The match had already begun and two goals had been scored, yet the referee agreed to ___ play until the storm passed.
   A) postpone
   B) suspend
   C) abandon
   D) cancel

26. Because the two maps came from one survey, their coastlines should ___; where they ___, a clerk has made a copying error.
   A) combine … differ
   B) differ … align
   C) align … combine
   D) align … differ

27. The manual clearly warns against overloading the shelf; even so, the workers chose to ___ the instruction.
   A) forget
   B) ignore
   C) doubt
   D) avoid

28. Since the bridge had collapsed and no boat was available, the river itself became the main ___ to their journey.
   A) objection
   B) restriction
   C) boundary
   D) obstacle

29. Food vanishes from the high meadow in winter, and several small mammals, such as the ground squirrel, ___ rather than move to lower ground.
   A) settle
   B) migrate
   C) retreat
   D) hibernate

30. Since the bonus reaches every worker whether the quota is met or not, the scheme leaves no real ___ to work faster.
   A) incentive
   B) prize
   C) reward
   D) privilege

31. The grant cannot remove the shortage of housing; moreover, it is far too small to ___ the crowding in the older districts.
   A) suppress
   B) alleviate
   C) cure
   D) eliminate

32. The lecture was clear and well ordered; furthermore, the speaker used simple diagrams to ___ each new term.
   A) demonstrate
   B) decorate
   C) illustrate
   D) exaggerate

33. The valley is fertile, so the terraces ___ a heavy crop, and the growers then ___ it before the autumn rains ruin the grain.
   A) yield … cultivate
   B) yield … harvest
   C) harvest … yield
   D) cultivate … harvest

34. Because the paint was still wet, the crew agreed to ___ the opening by two days.
   A) prevent
   B) cancel
   C) delay
   D) interrupt

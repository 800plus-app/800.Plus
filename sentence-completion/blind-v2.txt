# 8 sentence-completion items. Choose the ONE option that best completes each sentence.
# Two blanks (___ ... ___) means the option supplies both words, in order.

1. The car started every morning, so the mechanic looked for a small ___ in the wiring rather than a broken cable.
   A) fault
   B) waste
   C) place
   D) break

2. Because two centuries of grazing stripped the slope, the valley now bakes without shade or water, and travellers call it a ___.
   A) estate
   B) desert
   C) jungle
   D) territory

3. He apologised at length, but the committee doubted his ___; furthermore, he repeated the same conduct the following week.
   A) sincerity
   B) confession
   C) virtue
   D) conduct

4. She had given her word and everyone expected her to keep it; instead, she chose to ___ the promise without a word of explanation.
   A) abandon
   B) break
   C) betray
   D) forsake

5. The drug does not merely ease the symptoms for a few hours; taken daily, it can actually ___ the illness.
   A) remedy
   B) relieve
   C) treat
   D) cure

6. Unless the images improve, the laboratory cannot ___ the chemical composition, even though the crystals themselves are already clearly visible.
   A) survey
   B) analyze
   C) observe
   D) examine

7. Every fact in the deed has already been checked and found true; even so, it carries no legal force until the registrar agrees to ___ it.
   A) confirm
   B) authorize
   C) verify
   D) examine

8. He did not give up the theory; he chose only to ___ it from the journal until the new data arrived.
   A) abandon
   B) withdraw
   C) reject
   D) dismiss
